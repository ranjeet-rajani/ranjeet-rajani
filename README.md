## Projects

### 🏥 [Pharma Commercial Analytics Command Center](https://github.com/ranjeet-rajani/pharma-Commercial-Analytics-command-center)

`Power BI` · `DAX` · `SQL` · `Power Query` · `Star Schema Modeling`

A seven-page executive report that traces a **$4.34M revenue shortfall** to its operational root causes and assigns each one an owner.

- 9-table star schema, 16 single-direction relationships, **45 DAX measures** reconciled to source
- Found that **30% of the gap ($1.31M) traced to inventory stockouts**, not sales execution
- Classified all 20 territories by root cause — supply, competitive, or execution — each mapped to an accountable function

> The interesting finding wasn't *who* missed quota. Every territory did. It was *why* — because seven of them didn't have product on the shelf, and coaching those teams would have achieved nothing.

### 🚲 [Cyclistic Bike-Share Analysis](https://github.com/ranjeet-rajani/cyclistic-bike-share-analysis)

`R` · `tidyverse` · `ggplot2` · `Google Data Analytics Capstone`

Analysis of **4.3M bike-share rides** comparing casual riders and annual members to support a membership conversion strategy.

- Members ride **1.8× more often**; casual riders ride **1.9× longer**
- Casual ridership swings **8× seasonally** against under 3× for members
- Five visualizations and three targeted marketing recommendations
